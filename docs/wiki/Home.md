# Image Colour Palette Generator

🌐 **English** · [Dansk](Home-da) · [Bahasa Melayu](Home-ms)

Day 92 assignment of the course *100 Days of Code: The Complete Python Pro
Bootcamp*. A small website where you upload an image and see its ten most
common colours with their HEX codes, computed with NumPy.
